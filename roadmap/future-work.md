# Future Work

This document outlines potential directions for expansion, long-term research topics, and areas for future improvement of the Empus project.

---

## 1. Advanced Network Protocols
- Explore integration of emerging transport protocols beyond current Go implementations.
- Evaluate performance and resilience of QUIC extensions and hybrid models.

## 2. Enhanced Security Layers
- Continuous review of cryptographic primitives.
- Research into post-quantum encryption methods.
- Expand auditing frameworks for both client and server.

## 3. Storage Innovations
- Integration with decentralized storage networks (IPFS, Arweave, Filecoin).
- Research into redundancy and mirroring strategies.
- Long-term archival solutions for critical data.

## 4. Distribution Improvements
- Multi-path content delivery with adaptive fallback mechanisms.
- Exploration of peer-to-peer distribution models.
- Optimize for low-bandwidth and high-latency environments.

## 5. User Interface Evolution
- Continuous refinement of UI/UX for accessibility.
- Integration of modular UI components for different user roles.
- Research into privacy-preserving analytics for UI optimization.

## 6. Emergency Channel Expansion
- Explore additional output channels for resilience.
- Research into automated failover and escalation protocols.
- Long-term monitoring of geopolitical and regulatory risks.

## 7. Organizational Growth
- Expansion of developer team as project scales.
- Establish partnerships with academic and industry research groups.
- Continuous evaluation of legal and compliance frameworks.

---

## Conclusion
The future work outlined here provides a roadmap for sustained innovation and resilience. Each area represents opportunities to strengthen the Empus system, ensuring it remains adaptable to technological, regulatory, and societal changes.
