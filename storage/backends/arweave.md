
# Arweave Storage

Arweave provides permanent, immutable storage for long‑term archiving.  
It ensures historical integrity and verifiable persistence.

---

## Characteristics
- One‑time storage cost for permanent availability  
- Immutable records prevent tampering  
- Guarantees long‑term availability and verifiable history  
- Ideal for censorship‑resistant environments  

---

## Integration
- Router selects Arweave for critical or archival content  
- Schema ensures versioning and metadata preservation  
- Health checks validate immutability and availability
