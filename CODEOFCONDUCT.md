# Code of Conduct

This project aims to create a safe, respectful, and collaborative environment for contributors and users.  
Because this system is designed for high‑risk environments, additional care is required to protect user safety and maintain project integrity.

---

## Our Standards

We commit to:
- Respectful and professional communication  
- Constructive and actionable feedback  
- Inclusive and open participation  
- Awareness of security and regional risks  
- Collaboration that prioritizes user safety  

We do not tolerate:
- Harassment or discrimination  
- Personal attacks or hostility  
- Threats, intimidation, or coercion  
- Behavior that endangers users in high‑risk regions  
- Sharing sensitive information without consent  

---

## Responsibilities

### Contributors must:
- Maintain a respectful tone  
- Consider security implications in all actions  
- Protect user privacy and safety  
- Follow contribution guidelines and review processes  
- Avoid introducing region‑specific risks or metadata leaks  

### Maintainers must:
- Review contributions fairly and consistently  
- Enforce this Code of Conduct  
- Address unacceptable behavior promptly  
- Protect the project from harmful or unsafe changes  
- Maintain confidentiality when handling sensitive reports  

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:
- Report privately using the designated contact method  
- Provide relevant details (without exposing sensitive metadata)  
- Anonymous reports are allowed and respected  

Reports will be handled discreetly to protect all parties involved.

---

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- Verbal or written warnings  
- Temporary participation restrictions  
- Rejection of contributions  
- Permanent removal from the project  
- Escalation to platform administrators if necessary  

Enforcement decisions are made by maintainers with user safety as the primary concern.

---

## Appeals

If you believe an enforcement action was incorrect or unfair, you may request a review.  
Appeals will be evaluated by maintainers not involved in the original decision when possible.

---

## Commitment

We strive to maintain a safe, respectful, and supportive environment for everyone involved.  
All participants share responsibility for protecting users—especially those operating under hostile or high‑risk conditions.
